# Official Colors, Embroidery Specifications & Pattern Usage

Authoritative color specifications, embroidery thread conversion standards, Legacy Mark palette definitions, and Adidas pattern usage combinations compiled from official East Carolina University (ECU) Athletics and Trademark Licensing publications:

- **[2023 ECU Athletics Brand Guide](site-upload-content/2023_ECU_Athletics_Brand_Guide.pdf)**: Page 24 (Official Colors & Adidas Pattern) and Page 33 (Legacy Mark Standards).
- **[ECU Trademark Licensing Art Sheet](src/art-sheet-5-8-23/2023-05-08-art-sheet-01.svg)**: Colors Section ([`fmt/svg/colors-section.svg`](fmt/svg/colors-section.svg)).

## Overview

The East Carolina University (ECU) visual identity is anchored by its core university colors—**Purple** and **Gold**—supplemented by defined **Accent Colors** (Black, White, and Crimson/Red), a specialized **Legacy Mark Palette** (incorporating Beige/Skin Tone `PMS 728`), commercial **Embroidery Thread Standards** (Madeira & Robison-Anton), and designated **Adidas Pattern** combinations:

- **Core Palette**: ECU Purple (`PMS 268 C/U`) and ECU Gold (`PMS 109 U` / `PMS 123 C`).
- **Accent Colors**: Black (`Process Black C`), White, and Crimson / Red (`PMS 201 C`).
- **Legacy Mark Palette**: ECU Purple, ECU Gold, and Pirate Skin Tone / Beige (`PMS 728 C/U`) for the historic 1998 Pirate Head mark.
- **Commercial Embroidery Thread Standards**: Direct thread number conversions for Madeira Rayon/Polyneon and Robison-Anton (RA) thread systems across all seven standard colors.
- **Adidas Pattern Combinations**: Five approved two-tone foreground/background combinations for geometric brand textures across athletic apparel and uniforms.

> [!NOTE]
> Physical color reproduction on coated and uncoated paper stocks, apparel fabrics, and equipment requires adherence to the designated PANTONE® Matching System (PMS) standards. Digital (RGB/HEX) and four-color process (CMYK) values are calibrated to reproduce these official standards on screen and in print.

## Detailed Color Specifications

### 1. Official Colors (Core Palette)

The primary color palette comprises **ECU Purple** and **ECU Gold**. ECU Gold provides separate standardized formulas for uncoated paper/apparel substrates (`PMS 109 U`) and coated paper stocks (`PMS 123 C`).

| Color | PANTONE® (PMS) | CMYK Values | RGB Values | HEX Code | Madeira | Robison-Anton (RA) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: |
| **Purple** | `PMS 268 C`<br>`PMS 268 U` | `C=82 M=98 Y=0 K=12` | `R=88 G=44 B=131` | `#582C83` | `1122` | `2254` |
| **Gold** (Uncoated) | `PMS 109 U` | `C=0 M=11 Y=96 K=0` | `R=255 G=199 B=0` | `#FFC700` | `1024` | `2242` |
| **Gold** (Coated) | `PMS 123 C` | `C=0 M=22 Y=83 K=0` | `R=255 G=199 B=44` | `#FFC72C` | `1024` | `2558` |

> [!TIP]
> **Apparel & Manufacturing Rule**: Gold imprint, embroidery, and non-apparel (non-paper) products must match **PMS 109 U** across all physical manufacturing.

### 3. Legacy Mark (Pirate Head) Palette

A Pirate Head graphic has traditionally represented ECU Athletics dating back to the adoption of Pirates as the school mascot. First implemented as the primary athletic mark in **1998**, this legacy mark represents the history and tradition of Pirate Athletics, bridging the program's past and future.

- **Authorized Venues**: Hall of Fame displays, letterwinners recognitions, tradition areas, and historical retrospective endeavors.
- **Co-Branding**: Can be paired in association with other official ECU identifiers only with prior administrative licensing approval.
- **Color Specifications**: Combines the core Purple and Gold palette with a designated skin tone (**PANTONE® 728** / Beige).

## Pattern Usage Color Combinations

The East Carolina Athletics brand system incorporates a specialized compass-rose / star pattern (Adidas Pattern). Page 24 establishes **five approved two-tone color combinations** governing the relationship between the foreground pattern motif and the background field:

| Combination | Pattern (Foreground) | Field (Background) | Pattern HEX | Field HEX | Description |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Gold** | **Purple** | `#FFC700` / `#FFC72C` | `#582C83` | Primary athletic contrast pairing (Gold motif on Purple field) |
| **2** | **Purple** | **Gold** | `#582C83` | `#FFC700` / `#FFC72C` | Reverse core pairing (Purple motif on Gold field) |
| **3** | **Black** | **Purple** | `#000000` | `#582C83` | Tonal / stealth pairing (Black motif on Purple field) |
| **4** | **White** | **Purple** | `#FFFFFF` | `#582C83` | High-contrast neutral pairing (White motif on Purple field) |
| **5** | **Black** | **Crimson** | `#000000` | `#9D2235` | High-impact accent pairing (Black motif on Crimson field) |

> [!IMPORTANT]
>
> - Pattern color combinations are restricted to the five pairings enumerated above. Unapproved color substitutions, inversions, or multi-color gradients are strictly prohibited.
> - High contrast between the foreground pattern and background field must always be preserved for visual clarity and brand integrity.
