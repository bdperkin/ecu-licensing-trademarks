# ECU Athletics College Vault Program & Vintage Marks

Official brand standards, historical classifications, and licensing policies for the East Carolina University (ECU) **College Vault** program and its vintage visual identity assets, integrating specifications from page 38 of the [2023 ECU Athletics Brand Guide](../../../site-upload-content/2023_ECU_Athletics_Brand_Guide.pdf).

---

## College Vault Program Overview

The **College Vault** is an exclusive, vintage-inspired merchandise and identity program created to honor East Carolina University’s historical trademarks, athletic heritage, and campus traditions through curated, fashion-forward apparel and non-apparel products.

- **Program Mission**: The College Vault enables ECU to celebrate protected vintage marks representing the university's storied past within a trendy, modern lifestyle collection that is clearly differentiated from current on-field team branding in the retail marketplace.
- **Brand Positioning**: While contemporary ECU Athletics programs are anchored by the primary Jolly Roger Skull & Crossbones and slab-serif word marks, Vault marks serve historical, commemorative, and lifestyle retail roles.

---

## Approved Applications & Uniform Rules

Per official athletics brand guidelines (Page 38), College Vault marks are approved strictly for the following uses:

1. **Retail & E-Commerce Merchandise**: Primary deployment across fashion apparel, headwear, retro outerwear, and commemorative novelty items produced by authorized licensees.
2. **Throwback Games & Uniforms**: Authorized for official athletic throwback games honoring specific historical teams, seasons, or milestone anniversaries.
3. **Alternative Game Uniforms**: Permitted for use on **one designated alternative game uniform** per athletic season with Department of Athletics and Licensing Office approval.

> [!IMPORTANT]
>
> - Any use of College Vault marks on game uniforms, coaches' sideline wear, or practice gear must receive advance administrative review and written authorization from the ECU Office of Licensing and Trademarks.
> - Vault marks must not be blended, merged, or co-branded with modern primary athletics marks without explicit approval.

## Approved College Vault Vintage Marks

Per East Carolina University’s official athletics branding and trademark guidelines, three core vintage marks are showcased in the College Vault collection (Page 38):

### 1. Traditional Pirate Head with Sword (`ecu-traditional-pirate-head.svg`)

- **Design Classification**: The vintage **Traditional Pirate Head with Sword** (historically categorized in early extractions as `use792.png`).
- **Visual Description**: Features a front-facing pirate face gripping a cutlass / dagger horizontally between clenched teeth, wearing a purple tricorn hat bearing the skull-and-crossbones emblem and gold accent trim.
- **Historical Significance**: Adopted as a primary athletics identifier in 1998 and preserved as an official heritage mark to honor the university's long-standing athletic history.
- **Trademark Designator**: Trademark (`™`).
- **Asset Link**: [`ecu-traditional-pirate-head.svg`](ecu-traditional-pirate-head.svg)

### 2. Vintage Full-Body Walking PeeDee Mascot (`ecu-vintage-peedee-fullbody.svg`)

- **Design Classification**: Vintage full-body mascot illustration of **PeeDee the Pirate** (historically referenced as `use793.png`).
- **Visual Description**: Full-body depiction of PeeDee marching forward in a purposeful stride with clenched fists, wearing a purple pirate coat, gold tricorn hat with block "ECU" lettering, black-and-white striped nautical shirt, black eyepatch, and gold trousers.
- **Historical Significance**: A classic mid-century and late-20th-century mascot illustration representing ECU's campus spirit. While modern branding utilizes the updated Jolly Roger or legacy head marks, this full-body illustration remains a protected vintage asset.
- **Trademark Designator**: Trademark (`™`).
- **Asset Link**: [`ecu-vintage-peedee-fullbody.svg`](ecu-vintage-peedee-fullbody.svg)

### 3. Interlocking "EC" Block Letters (`ecu-block-letters.svg`)

- **Design Classification**: The **ECU Block Letters** or **Interlocking EC Mark** (historically referenced as `use794.png`).
- **Visual Description**: Heavy block-slab serif "E" interlocked with a matching block-slab serif "C", rendered in solid official ECU Purple (`#582C83`).
- **Historical Significance**: Served as a prominent institutional and secondary athletics logotype across historic uniforms, baseball caps, equipment, and varsity apparel.
- **Trademark Designator**: Trademark (`™`).
- **Asset Link**: [`ecu-block-letters.svg`](ecu-block-letters.svg)
