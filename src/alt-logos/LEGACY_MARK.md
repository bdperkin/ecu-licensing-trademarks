# ECU Athletics Legacy Mark (Pirate Head)

Comprehensive brand guidelines, historical context, usage regulations, and reproduction standards for the East Carolina University (ECU) Athletics **Legacy Mark (Pirate Head)**, compiled directly from pages 33 and 34 of the [2023 ECU Athletics Brand Guide](site-upload-content/2023_ECU_Athletics_Brand_Guide.pdf).

---

## Historical Heritage & Identity

A Pirate Head graphic has traditionally represented East Carolina University Athletics dating back to the university's adoption of the "Pirates" moniker as its athletic mascot.

- **Adoption as Primary Identity (1998)**: This specific three-quarter profile pirate head design was formally adopted and implemented as the university's primary athletic mark in **1998**.
- **Role in the Modern Brand System**: While contemporary athletics branding centers on the Jolly Roger Skull & Crossbones primary mark, this 1998 design serves as the university's official **Legacy Mark**. It represents the storied history and tradition of the ECU athletics program, serving as an emotional anchor tying the program's past and future together.
- **Trademark Status**: Fully protected registered trademark (`®`), with the registration indicator affixed at the bottom-right contour below the chin.

---

## Approved Applications & Usage Restrictions

Due to its heritage status, use of the Legacy Mark is deliberately curated and governed by strict athletic department policies:

### 1. Authorized Venues & Programs

The Legacy Mark is specifically designated for use in:

- **ECU Athletics Hall of Fame** exhibits, plaques, and displays.
- **Letterwinners Recognition**: Letterwinners clubs, reunions, senior awards, and varsity letter jacket programs.
- **Tradition & Heritage Areas**: Stadium and arena heritage corridors, historical retrospective timelines, and commemorative athletics displays.
- **Historical Endeavors**: Special anniversary publications, archival films, alumni tributes, and retrospective merchandise recognizing the history of Pirate Athletics.

### 2. Co-Branding & Association Regulations

- **Administrative Approval**: The Legacy Mark can only be used in association with other official ECU identifiers (such as the primary Skull & Crossbones, sport-specific lockups, or primary word marks) with **prior written administrative approval** from the ECU Office of Licensing and Trademarks.
- **Integrity of the Mark**: No alterations to head proportions, bandana knotting, or facial features are permitted. The mark must always retain its outer boundary outline and contrast backgrounds.

## Visual Mark Architecture

Page 34 of the brand guide establishes the standalone visual representation of the mark:

- **Tricorn Hat**: ECU Purple tricorn cap with deep black interior crease shadows and an accent gold outer contour.
- **Bandana & Knot**: ECU Gold bandana wrapped snugly across the brow with two flowing knot tails trailing to the left.
- **Eye Patch**: Classic black pirate eye patch angled across the right eye (viewer's left).
- **Facial Features**: Expressive left eye gazing forward, arched eyebrow, prominent nose, and gold hoop earring affixed to the left ear.
- **Beard & Moustache**: Bold solid black beard, mustache, and sideburns framing the jawline.
- **Skin Tone**: Warm tan/beige complexion (`PMS 728`) applied across facial contours, nose, brow, and ear, shaded with rich purple undertones.
- **Contour Border**: Encapsulated by a bold ECU Gold silhouette border ensuring separation against dark or complex backgrounds.
- **Trademark Designator**: Registered trademark symbol (`®`) in solid black enclosed in a circle at the lower right.

## Manufacturing & Reproduction Rules

> [!IMPORTANT]
>
> - **Apparel & Non-Paper Manufacturing**: Gold imprint, embroidery, tackle twill, and non-paper products must match **PMS 109 U**.
> - **Skin Tone Fidelity**: The skin tone must match **PMS 728 C** (coated) or **PMS 728 U** (uncoated). Substituting unapproved flesh or peach tones compromises brand integrity and is strictly prohibited.
> - **Screen vs. Print Verification**: Digital RGB and HEX values are intended for on-screen representation and should never replace physical PANTONE® Color Standards for dye lots and textile production.
