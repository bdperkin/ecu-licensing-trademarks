# Site Upload Content (`site-upload-content/`)

This directory contains the original, raw reference documents, PDF publications, and extracted vector/raster assets harvested directly from official East Carolina University (ECU) licensing and trademark resources.

## File Summaries

### 1. Primary Reference Publications

- **`2023_ECU_Athletics_Brand_Guide.pdf`**: The authoritative 43-page guide published in June 2023 establishing visual standards, primary and secondary logos, sport-specific lockups, typography rules (Gotham and Matrix font families), uniform color combinations, and mascot guidelines.
- **`Art-Sheet-5.8.23.pdf`**: The official 3-page consolidated trademark art sheet (revision date May 8, 2023) listing all 91 approved university marks, word marks, brand patterns, color swatch specifications, and licensing guidelines.

### 2. Split Assets & Vector Extracts

- **`2023-ecu-athletics-brand-guide-pdf/` & `2023-ecu-athletics-brand-guide-svg/`**: Individual page-by-page extractions of the 43-page brand guide, providing granular access to specific sections such as color palettes, uniform numbers, and sport typography.
- **`art-sheet-5-8-23-pdf/` & `art-sheet-5-8-23-svg/`**: The raw, unnormalized vector extract of the 3-page consolidated art sheet that served as the base input for the curated SVG in [`src/art-sheet-5-8-23/`](../src/art-sheet-5-8-23/).

### 3. Source Link Catalog

- **`extracted_links.txt`**: A curated catalog of source URLs referencing official ECU portals, brand guidelines, licensing policy pages, and document download endpoints.
